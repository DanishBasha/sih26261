# AICTE Handbook Requirements (2024–25 to 2026–27)

**Source Document:** AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) 2024–25 to 2026–27  
**Official File Reference:** `2025031399.pdf` (231 pages)  
**Problem Statement:** SIH26261  

## 1. Scope & Chapters Covered
- **Chapter I:** New Technical Institution Establishment (p. 1 onwards).
- **Chapter II:** Extension of Approval (EoA) for Existing Institutions (p. 11 onwards).
- **Chapter VI:** Standard Norms for Technical Education.
- **Chapter VII:** Penal actions and deficiencies.
- **Chapter VIII:** Do's and Don'ts for applicant institutions.

## 2. Key Annexures
- **Annexure 1 (New Institutions):** Mandatory documents including building plan, registered architect certificate (Certificate 1), approved building plan (Certificate 2), bank certificate (Certificate 3), resolution in Format 3, fire safety certificate, registered land deed/lease (min 30 years), and Section 8 company constitution.
- **Annexure 2 (Existing Institutions / EoA):** Annual affidavits, statutory compliance proofs, payment receipts, structural stability certificates (for buildings older than specified threshold), fire certificates, and previous year's audited accounts.

## 3. Human Scrutiny Principle
AI/OCR scrutiny acts as a decision-support aid. Every extracted flag is grounded in an explicit handbook clause reference, but official decisions remain strictly with authorized scrutiny committees.
